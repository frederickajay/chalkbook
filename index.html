Chalkbook

Your shop's tab, minus the paper. Who owes you, and since when? Chalkbook answers in one glance.

What it does

  • Tap Gave when someone takes goods on credit, Got when they pay. Every customer and supplier has a running balance.
  • Send a friendly WhatsApp or SMS reminder in the tone you choose. Customers agree first and can reply STOP.
  • Make a bill or receipt in seconds, share it or print it.
  • Attach a voice note or a photo to any entry.
  • Charts show what you are owed and who has owed the longest.
  • Works fully offline. Your book stays on your phone, locked with Face ID or your passcode.
  • Export everything to a spreadsheet for free, any time.

Honest by design

No ads. We do not sell your customers' data. The credit book is free forever; Chalkbook Pro is an optional subscription for an Excel report and branded bills. Chalkbook does not file taxes or hold money.
