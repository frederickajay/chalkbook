Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone only. Chalkbook has no account in this version and does not upload your book. If you lose or replace your phone, you need a backup you saved yourself (More → backup section). Without one we cannot recover your data.

How do I back up and restore?

In More, the backup section creates an encrypted file protected by a PIN you choose (6 or more digits). Save it in Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, use "Restore from backup" in More on the new phone and pick the file. If you forget the PIN, the backup cannot be opened.

How do I export my data?

More → "Export to spreadsheet (CSV)" gives a CSV that opens in Excel, free and unlimited.

How do I send a reminder?

Open a customer, tap Remind, pick a tone and send it through your own WhatsApp or SMS. Send reminders only to customers who agreed. If someone says STOP, mark them as opted out.

Subscription (Chalkbook Pro)

Pro is billed by Apple. Manage or cancel it at Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To get your purchase back on a new phone, open More → Chalkbook Pro → Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my data

Delete the app, or use "Delete all data" in More. Because nothing is stored on our servers, that removes everything.
