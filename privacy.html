Chalkbook privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. Chalkbook is provided by Frederick Mahil, 1/338, 3rd Street, Adhithanar Nagar, Iyyapanthangal, Chennai 600056, Tamil Nadu, India ("we"). Contact: frederickajay@gmail.com.

Short version

Your credit book stays on your phone. We do not collect it, we do not sell data, there are no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores on your phone

Names, phone numbers, amounts, dates and notes of your customers and suppliers; optional photos and voice notes you attach; your shop details and settings. This is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS data protection and, if you turn it on, Face ID or your passcode.

What we collect

In this version, nothing. The app makes no network requests of its own and has no account. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Purchases are processed by Apple; we receive no payment details and no personal data from them.

Permissions

  • Face ID / passcode: optional app lock. The app never sees your biometrics.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to record voice notes and turn speech into a draft entry. Recognition runs on the phone.
  • Photos and Contacts: only the items you pick in the system picker. We do not read your address book or photo library.

Sharing you start

When you share a reminder, bill, statement or backup, it goes through the app you choose (WhatsApp, SMS, Mail, Files). That app's own privacy policy applies. You must have your customers' agreement before messaging them, and must respect STOP requests.

Backups and export

Backups are encrypted with a key derived from your PIN and saved where you choose. We cannot read them or recover a lost PIN.

Future features

If we add optional online features (sign-in, sync, hosted statement links, anonymous statistics), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before they are enabled, and ask for your consent where the law requires.

Your rights and retention

Because your data is only on your device, you control it: edit or delete entries in the app, export a CSV, or delete the app to remove everything. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, Kenya's Data Protection Act, Nigeria's NDPR or India's DPDP Act) you may have further rights; contact us and we will help, although we hold no copy of your data.

Your customers' data

You decide what to record about your customers and are responsible for having a lawful basis, such as their agreement, to keep it and to contact them. Record only what you need.

Children

Chalkbook is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country).

Changes

We will post changes here with a new effective date.
